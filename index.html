👋 Hi, I'm Oisín. I'm currently based in Eindhoven, The Netherlands, where I've been working on active perception in ultrasound imaging, as part of my PhD. Our lab is aiming to understand how we can enable ultrasound machines to decide for themselves how they should scan each patient to best achieve their diagnostic task. We believe that this will lead to cheaper, higher quality scans that make ultrasound more useful and more accessible. For more information on this topic, check out our papers on cognitive ultrasound.
